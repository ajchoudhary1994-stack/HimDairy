Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information HimDairy collects when you place an order, and how we use it. We collect only what's needed to deliver fresh dairy to your door.

Information we collect

  • Your name, phone number and delivery address, entered in our order form.
  • Your chosen products, quantities and delivery date/time.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, collected automatically for site performance.

How we use it

  • To confirm and fulfil your order via WhatsApp or phone.
  • To coordinate delivery logistics and, where opted in, recurring subscriptions.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

How your order reaches us

When you tap "Send Order on WhatsApp", your order details are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device, which you then choose to send to HimDairy's WhatsApp number. This message is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy once sent.

Data retention

We retain order details only as long as needed for delivery, accounting and customer support purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us to review, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by contacting us at hello@himdairy.in.

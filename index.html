Our Story
Born in the mountains,
made for your table

HimDairy started in 2023 when a group of Himachali farmers realized the world was paying premium prices for "organic" dairy — but not a rupee was reaching the actual mountains where it came from.

We built a direct bridge. No middlemen. No cold storage chains that kill freshness. Just farmers, their cows, and your doorstep.
